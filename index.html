Taige Luo

M.S. Student in Geography

I am Taige Luo from Nanjing, Jiangsu, China. I am currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Geography at Virginia Tech under the supervision of Dr. Yang Shao. My current research interests focus on urban and vegetation remote sensing applications in the era of artificial intelligence, with a broader interest in how AI can advance ecological and Earth system research. Methodologically, my work involves large-scale remote sensing data processing and fusion, as well as advanced AI models, including computer vision, vision-language models, and large language models. Before joining Virginia Tech, I completed my undergraduate studies at Nanjing Forestry University, where I worked with Dr. Sheng Xu. My undergraduate research focused on intelligent vegetation parameter extraction from low-altitude UAV imagery, including fine-grained individual tree crown detection, individual tree density estimation, and leaf phenotyping. Overall, my research lies at the intersection of remote sensing, artificial intelligence, and ecological applications.

I am actively seeking potential collaboration opportunities and Ph.D. positions. If you are interested in working with me, please feel free to contact me at: taige@vt.edu.
